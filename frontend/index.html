Limitations & future work

The catalogue is intentionally small and curated (~60 tracks): synthetic fixtures for reproducible evaluation plus hand-picked real recordings for a more recognisable demo. Scoring still uses local mood/activity/genre tags — not streaming history or collaborative filtering.

A sample of real tracks was looked up against MusicBrainz and cached in musicbrainz_cache. Enrichment is optional, rate-limited, and fails open; it does not drive ranking yet. See docs/LIMITATIONS.md and docs/enrichment_sample.json for the attempt and results.

  • Scale catalogue ingestion while keeping privacy (no user history in DB).
  • Use cached MusicBrainz tags as soft scoring signals when available.
  • Add audio-feature or playlist continuity signals beyond metadata tags.
